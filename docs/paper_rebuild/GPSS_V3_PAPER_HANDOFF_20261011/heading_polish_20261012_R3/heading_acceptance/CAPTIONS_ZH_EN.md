# 图5：短基线方向观测的航向误差与输出支持

**短基线方向处理路线在四足机器人记录中的航向误差。** (a)大幅机身运动记录中，位置产品衍生方向输入、原生更新实际采用的方向以及默认 RTKLIB 动基线输出；右侧为既定 80–100 s 转弯放大。(b) C-LAMBDA 与 C-WLS 的原始输出和已固定 ratio ≥ 3 工程筛选后输出的绝对航向误差，两个方法分别展示。全部有参考支持的历元逐点显示，箱体为四分位区间，黑线为中位数，须线延伸到 1.5 倍四分位距范围内的实际样本；范围外的样本仍显示。水平方向抖动仅用于防止覆盖。数字为输出数／完整评价窗历元数。(c–e)三条完整评价记录的绝对航向误差经验累积分布，主轴展开 0–15°，内嵌图保留完整 0–180°，纵轴均为0–1。C-LAMBDA/C-WLS 虚线为原始结果，实线为已完成的 ratio 筛选后结果。本文蓝线是采用的方向观测，不是融合后的姿态。本轮未重新筛选或改变任何原始误差及支持。

**Heading errors of short-baseline direction-processing routes on the quadruped recordings.** (a) Position-derived direction before and after native selection, with default RTKLIB moving-baseline outputs during the large-body-motion recording; the adjacent detail shows the previously selected turn at 80–100 s. (b) Absolute heading errors before and after the previously fixed ratio ≥ 3 screen, with C-LAMBDA and C-WLS shown separately. Every finite reference-supported epoch is plotted. Boxes show the interquartile range, black lines the medians, and whiskers extend to observations within 1.5 interquartile ranges. Samples beyond the whiskers remain visible. Horizontal jitter is for display only. Counts are outputs over all recorded evaluation epochs. (c–e) Full-record empirical cumulative distributions of absolute heading error: the main axes show 0–15° and insets the complete 0–180° range, both with cumulative scale 0–1. For C-LAMBDA/C-WLS, dashed curves denote original estimates and solid curves the completed project-added ratio screen. The proposed curve shows selected direction observations rather than fused navigation attitude. This revision changes only the display; errors, support and acceptance states are unchanged.
