# Fig. 8

观测质量下降时来源加权的条件性航向收益。代表回放对位置加入水平／垂直标准差为3/5 m的噪声，保留原自报精度。(a) 记录的位置—预测差与名义标准差范数。(b) 同一前120 s中实际协方差倍率，点、粗线、细线分别为中位数、四分位区间、全范围；倍率越大，该来源相对基准的影响越小。(c) 关闭和启用来源加权的航向误差，整274 s指标同时保留位置代价。该对照改变整个来源加权策略，不隔离单个来源。在513个共同完成案例中，190例航向RMSE降低、323例升高，中位降低量为−0.000258°；541个登记案例另有6例仅完整方法完成。完整变体、失效和原时序随配套材料保留。

Conditional heading-robustness benefit of source-dependent weighting under degraded observations. The retained replay adds horizontal/vertical position-noise standard deviations of 3/5 m while preserving the reported uncertainty. (a) Recorded position–prediction mismatch and nominal standard-deviation norm. (b) Actual covariance multipliers over the same first 120 s: points, thick bars and thin bars denote medians, interquartile ranges and full ranges. Larger covariance reduces relative influence within each source. (c) Heading errors with weighting disabled and enabled; complete 274-s metrics retain the positioning cost. The contrast changes the combined weighting policy, not an isolated source. Among 513 jointly completed cases, 190 have lower yaw RMSE and 323 higher; the median reduction is −0.000258°. Six additional cases complete only with the full method out of 541 registered cases. Complete outcomes and histories accompany the figure.
