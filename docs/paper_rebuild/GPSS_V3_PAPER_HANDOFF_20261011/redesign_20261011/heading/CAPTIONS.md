# 图 4：自身观测约束相对运动，保留全局方向自由度

**中文图注。** 同一组 IMU 与足端接触数据在不同初始航向下的估计结果。(a) 七次已有运行仅改变初始航向，得到相互旋转的路径；彩色突出 −100°、0°、+100°，其余四次以灰色显示，星号为共同起点。(b) 对每条路径只消除已知的初始航向旋转后，七条路径重合；没有拟合位置、尺度或时间偏移。(c) 一个预先按输入选定的双接触窗口内，七条航向增量曲线重合。(d) 五个预选接触窗口的已保存结构分析均保留四个未约束方向，即三维全局平移与一维全局航向；圆形和方形分别表示四接触和双接触窗口。该结果说明相同自身观测可以支持一致的相对运动，而不能单独确定全局方向。

**English caption.** Relative-motion agreement and global-heading freedom under proprioceptive sensing. (a) Seven archived runs of the same IMU and foot-contact measurements differ only in initial yaw and produce rotated trajectories. The −100°, 0°, and +100° runs are highlighted; the other four are gray. Stars mark the common start. (b) Removing only each run's known initial yaw makes the paths overlap, without fitting position, scale, or time. (c) Heading increments over an input-selected two-contact interval also overlap. (d) Saved structural analyses of five preselected contact intervals retain four unconstrained dimensions: three global translations and one global yaw. Circles and squares denote four- and two-contact windows. All 442,939 saved state samples are used in (a–b); the local interval contains 43 samples per run. Unresolved dimensions are counted at the original relative singular-value threshold of 10⁻⁹. Full spectra and threshold sensitivity remain in the source tables.

# 图 5：短基线方向处理路线的精度与可用性

**中文图注。** 三条实测记录中不同短基线方向处理路线的误差与可用性。(a–c) 纵轴为有效且有参考支持的方向观测 RMSE，横轴为整个评价窗中新有效方向的覆盖率。蓝色空心圆为本文的双位置短基线输入，蓝色星号为原生更新日志中实际采用的方向观测；连线显示观测选择前后的变化，星号不是滤波后验航向。橙色符号保留 RTKLIB 的全部四种配置；其余符号表示 C-LAMBDA、C-WLS 和 DD-KF。FAR/PAR 在三条原生记录中均无接受解，因此误差为 N/A。(d–f) 本文有效方向、实际采用方向以及 RTKLIB 默认配置的新固定输出时间。本文接纳计数为 1348/1370、1334/1350、1586/1885，分母是全窗 5 Hz 输入历元；可评分接纳观测分别为 1348、1333、1582。各路线使用各自的有效输出支持；C-LAMBDA/C-WLS 的方向候选没有整数接受检验。外部路线保留原评价结果，本文观测误差由冻结输入与既有参考序列在本轮配图层派生，未改变冻结结果表。

**English caption.** Accuracy and availability of short-baseline direction processing on three recorded trials. (a–c) Heading RMSE on valid, reference-supported directions versus new-output coverage over the complete evaluation window. Open blue circles show the proposed position-derived heading input; blue stars show input observations used by the archived estimator. Connecting lines indicate the effect of native observation selection; stars are not posterior heading estimates. Orange symbols retain all four RTKLIB configurations. Additional symbols show C-LAMBDA, C-WLS, and DD-KF. FAR and PAR accepted no solution in any trial, leaving their heading error unavailable. (d–f) Native times of available input, used observations, and default RTKLIB fixed outputs. Used-observation counts are 1348/1370, 1334/1350, and 1586/1885; their reference-supported counts are 1348, 1333, and 1582. Each processing route retains its own valid support. C-LAMBDA and C-WLS candidates have no integer acceptance test. External metrics are retained; input-observation errors are figure-layer calculations from frozen providers and the saved reference, without fitted bias or time shift. Full method counts, input levels, and matching rules accompany the figure.
