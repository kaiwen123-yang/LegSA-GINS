## 0. Measurand and evaluation chain

- Measurand per run: whole-window RMSE of heading (yaw), horizontal position and height at the antenna midpoint, against the fused navigation output of the commercial low-cost dual-antenna GNSS/INS receiver (Fixposition Vision-RTK 2), which the estimator under test does not read. Reference interpolated linearly at each retained estimator epoch; denominator = retained epochs [contract:340-365].
- Windows: BY2 66–340 s (274 s, 56 642 epochs), BY2H 413–683 s (270 s, 58 580), BY2O 3186–3563 s (377 s, 76 548) [contract:138-140; S5]. BY2O near-static float segment 3369.94–3411.95 s (7 612 epochs) [S7].
- Evaluation point: antenna midpoint = IMU point + C·[0.03, 0.03 − ½·b_med, −0.30] m (FRD), b_med/2 = 0.178 m on BY2; lever arm to GNSS1 [0.03, 0.03, −0.30] [contract:282; S2:452; PP:149].
- All 13 estimators (11 LegSA configurations, LC01, LC01-S) are evaluated on exactly matched epochs (56 628–76 548 common epochs per sequence, exact time match, no nearest-neighbour fallback needed) [S6].

## 2. Heading: decomposition and estimator-specific part

Whole-window heading error e(t) of each C00 run is decomposed into its mean (bias), a slow part (centered 5-s moving average minus the mean) and a fast part (remainder); bias² + slow² + fast² closes with the mean-square error to 1–8 % [S5] UNC_YAW_DECOMPOSITION.csv.

| Sequence | Row | RMSE | bias | slow std | fast std | shares bias/slow/fast | √(bias² + slow²) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BY2 | F04 | 1.886 | −1.086 | 0.816 | 1.305 | 33 / 19 / 48 % | 1.36 |
| BY2 | F02 | 2.232 | −1.390 | 1.153 | 1.307 | 39 / 27 / 34 % | 1.81 |
| BY2 | LC01 | 2.995 | −1.353 | 2.216 | 1.272 | 20 / 55 / 18 % | 2.60 |
| BY2 | LC01-S | 1.539 | −0.357 | 0.844 | 1.229 | 5 / 30 / 64 % | 0.92 |
| BY2H | F04 | 1.934 | −0.538 | 1.222 | 1.405 | 8 / 40 / 53 % | 1.34 |
| BY2H | LC01 | 2.209 | −0.805 | 1.445 | 1.391 | 13 / 43 / 40 % | 1.65 |
| BY2O | F04 | 2.434 | +0.339 | 2.076 | 1.078 | 2 / 73 / 20 % | 2.10 |
| BY2O | LC01 | 2.454 | +0.300 | 2.075 | 1.065 | 1 / 72 / 19 % | 2.10 |

Reading (replaces the 09-26 "lower bound" argument):

1. The fast part is common to all estimators of a sequence (row 2 of the budget) and vanishes when the robot is stationary; it is treated as an evaluation-chain term. Subtracting it in quadrature gives the estimator-specific heading error √(bias² + slow²): F04 1.36 / 1.34 / 2.10°.
2. The bias part differs between estimators on identical inputs (row 3): on BY2 LC01-S carries −0.36° where F04 carries −1.09°, and the two have almost the same random part (std 1.497 vs 1.542°); the 0.35° by which LC01-S beats F04 on BY2 is a bias difference. The bias is therefore attributed to the estimator's response to the uncorrected installation yaw, not to the reference, and it does not cancel between methods.
3. The slow part contains both the estimator's heading wander and the slow reference error (row 1). Under the independence assumption and the manufacturer figure (1.14°) the F04 estimator-specific part reduces further to 0.73 / 0.69° (BY2 / BY2H) and 1.77° (BY2O); with the receiver-reported 0.89–0.97° to 1.03 / 0.98 / 1.87°. Because the bias term is already attributed to installation, quoting these smaller values would double count; the manuscript should quote the decomposition (1.4° estimator-specific on BY2/BY2H, of which ≈ 1.1° / 0.5° is bias) rather than a single "true" estimator RMSE.
4. Paired comparisons: the reference and the fast term cancel exactly, the bias term does not. The mean-square difference is invariant to common terms; the RMSE difference and ratios are compressed by them (BY2 LC01 − F04: 1.109° in RMSE, 5.41 deg² in mean square). Report differences in degrees, never percentages.
5. BY2O: F04 and LC01 have the same decomposition on the full window (parity), but opposite behaviour by segment: inside the float segment F04 0.233° vs LC01 4.008°, outside F04 2.589° vs LC01 1.878° [S7]. The whole-window parity is the net of the two; write it by segment.

## 3. Position: decomposition, motion statistics

Horizontal error in the body frame (calibrated-chain evaluation at the v3 point, whose horizontal RMSE differs from the v3 chain by 0.4 mm) [S2 §10] UNC_HORIZONTAL_BODY_DECOMPOSITION.csv:

| Sequence | F04 horizontal RMSE | forward bias (share) | forward random (share) | right random (share) | height RMSE | height bias (share) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| BY2 | 0.098 | +0.044 m (20.4 %) | 0.078 m (62.4 %) | 0.041 m (17.2 %) | 0.049 | −0.017 m (12.0 %) |
| BY2H | 0.069 | +0.030 m (19.3 %) | 0.039 m (31.7 %) | 0.048 m (47.7 %) | 0.045 | −0.007 m (2.5 %) |
| BY2O | 0.056 | +0.028 m (25.0 %) | 0.041 m (53.1 %) | 0.026 m (21.9 %) | 0.046 | −0.019 m (16.8 %) |

- The forward bias is identical for all five LegSA configurations and for LC01 (+0.044 m at parity), i.e. an evaluation-chain term (row 5). Its timing equivalent (37 / 25 / 31 ms at the measured mean speeds [F]) is not a single constant, so it is not attributed to a time offset alone.
- Motion statistics [F]: yaw-rate RMS 18.0 / 17.5 / 15.4 °/s with |ω_z| > 10 °/s for 62 / 62 / 46 % of the window (gait oscillation); mean speed 1.19 / 1.20 / 0.89 m/s; BY2O stationary (< 0.2 m/s) for 24 % of the window. A 40 ms residual offset would contribute 0.72° RMS to heading and 0.048 m along track; the observed fast heading term (1.31°) and the forward bias (0.044 m) correspond to different equivalent offsets (≈ 72 ms vs 37 ms), which is why row 2 is reported as a common-mode term of undetermined origin.
- Reference position floor: with the pAcc lower bound the estimator-specific horizontal error on BY2 is 0.096 m; with the receiver-reported upper bound ≈ 0.07 m; with the along-track bias removed as well ≈ 0.05–0.09 m. The horizontal figures are therefore a parity check among the IMU-point methods (0.088–0.104 m on BY2), not the estimator's intrinsic accuracy.

## 7. Changes relative to the 2026-09-26 draft budget

1. New row 2 (common-mode fast heading term) and new row 3 (installation-yaw bias, estimator-dependent, not cancelling); row 1 now separates the slow/bias reference error from its short-term noise (≤ 0.06°).
2. Row 5 quantified from the sealed body-frame residuals; the "+40 ms" pilot is one consistent explanation among several and is no longer the headline.
3. Row 10 replaced assumed correlation times by measured intervals; the single-window heading uncertainty on BY2O (±1°) is far larger than assumed.
4. The 09-26 "lower bound" (F04 1.5–2.2°) is superseded by the decomposition of §2.
5. The provisional 0.1° / 0.3° distinguishability thresholds are withdrawn: the LC01 − F04 difference on BY2 (1.11°) is not resolved by the paired block bootstrap while the LC01-S − F04 difference (0.35°) is; verdicts are now per comparison (UNC-02).

## 8. Open items (limitations, no rerun)

- Origin of the common-mode fast heading term (row 2): a read-only cross-correlation of the fast heading error against the IMU yaw rate would separate time alignment from reference-side effects; not executed.
- Installation yaw and roll between IMU axes and antenna baseline (row 3): diagnostics recorded, chain frozen unchanged.
- Lever-arm vertical component (row 6): CAD verification open; bounded by the height residual to ≈ 0.02 m.
- Reference position uncertainty inside the BY2O float segment: the receiver's own dual-antenna baseline was not at its top state for 16–21 % of the odometry rows even on the nominal sequences [PP:212]; segment statistics are consistency values between two systems sharing the degraded observations.
