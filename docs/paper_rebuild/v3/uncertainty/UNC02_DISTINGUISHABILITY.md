# UNC-02 Distinguishability of the comparisons used in the manuscript

Basis: paired error series of the C00 runs on exactly matched epochs (UA01_C00_PAIRED_SERIES.csv @58d6d9c), 20-s moving-block bootstrap of the RMSE difference (B = 2000, seed 20260927), plus per-type seed statistics for the fault matrix. The full verdict table is UNC_DISTINGUISHABILITY.csv (56 rows, generated by scripts/paper_rebuild/unc02_derive_and_check.py); this note lists the rows the manuscript uses and the wording each supports.

## 1. Rule (fixed before the verdicts were read)

For a difference Δ = RMSE_A − RMSE_B on the same sequence and epochs, with the moving-block bootstrap 95 % interval [lo, hi] and the reporting resolution r (0.1° for heading, 5 mm for horizontal position):

- RESOLVED: the interval excludes zero and |Δ| ≥ r → "lower" / "higher", quote Δ and the interval.
- RESOLVED_NEGLIGIBLE: the interval excludes zero but |Δ| < r → "comparable"; the difference is below the reporting resolution.
- PARITY: the interval includes zero and |Δ| < r → "comparable".
- DIRECTION_ONLY: the interval includes zero and |Δ| ≥ r → state the sign and the interval, no "significant", no ratio, no percentage.

A fixed magnitude threshold does not work: the LC01 − F04 heading difference on BY2 (1.11°) is DIRECTION_ONLY because LC01's excess is carried by episodes of heading wander (squared-error correlation with F04 only 0.185), whereas the LC01-S − F04 difference (0.35°) is RESOLVED because it is a bias difference that is stable across blocks.

## 3. Segment structure of BY2O (write by segment, not by whole window)

UA01_BY2O_SEGMENT_BANDS.csv: heading RMSE F04 / LC01 = 0.233 / 4.008° inside the float segment (7 612 epochs), 1.862 / 6.174° in the secondary segment, 2.589 / 1.878° outside. The whole-window parity (2.434 vs 2.454°) is the net of F04 being better inside and LC01 being better outside; the manuscript text must say both.

## 4. Fault matrix (per-type seeds, nine per type)

- LC01 − F04 heading difference is positive for every seed of every type where both are finite (minimum 0.923°); under injected heading noise D32 / D33 / D35 it is 1.14 / 1.66 / 1.35° with SD 0.06 / 0.27 / 0.10° (UA01_EXTERNAL_PAIRED.csv).
- Position-outage family D62: F04 − F03 horizontal −1.61 m (SD 0.87) at 10 s and −10.5 m (SD 5.7) at 20 s, negative for 9/9 seeds; F04 − A04 parity (UA01_ADDENDUM_PAIRED.csv).
- Position-noise types D14/D21: LC01 and EXT05C diverge in 18/18 runs, F04 completes 18/18 (0/18 → 95 % CI of the failure rate [0, 0.19]).
- Failure rates of two types are distinguishable only when they differ by ≥ 5 of 9 seeds.

## 5. What the manuscript should not claim

- No "X % better" statements (ratios are compressed by the common-mode terms).
- No "significant" for the BY2 and BY2H LC01 comparisons; state Δ with the interval.
- No claim that RD, HV or SA improve nominal heading; their evidence is the D62 family and the D14/D21 completion rate.
- No whole-window BY2O comparison without the segment statement.
