# Notation and errata registry

No publisher erratum or attributable Yin code was found. Comparing the official 28-page VOR with the official 24-page v2 shows layout changes only; Equations 6–14 retain the same notation and omissions.

Eq. 8 defines `V_hat_k=L_k-H_k Xhat_(k/k-1)`. Eq. 9 does not use that hatted innovation: it uses `bar_V_k` throughout `Omega=bar_V_k^T bar_A_k bar_V_k+alpha_k bar_V_k^T bar_A_(Xhat_k) bar_V_k=min`. Neither `V_hat_k=bar_V_k` nor `L_k=Z_k` is stated, and `bar_A_(Xhat_k)` is undefined. The official versions, author response, and related patent preserve the symbol splits. Treating either pair as aliases is only `PAPER_DERIVED_POLICY_BASELINE`.

Eq. 6 prints `bar_V_k=H_k Xhat_k-Z_k` and `Delta_X_tilde_k=sqrt(bar_V_k^T bar_V_k / tr(P_(bar_V_k)))`. Its covariance line is exactly `P_(bar_V_k)=Phi_k P_(Xhat_(k-1)) Phi^T+Q_k`; the right `Phi` has no explicit `k` in the rendered/HTML formula. The author response confirms the barred quantity is a residual but does not resolve the `Xhat_k` stage. A 3D residual cannot be normalized dimensionlessly by a covariance described as belonging to the 21-state a-priori vector. Mapping it to `Phi_(k-1) P_(k-1)^plus Phi_(k-1)^T+Q_k`, replacing it by `H P^- H^T+R`, or adding normalization would each be an unselected `PAPER_DERIVED` completion.

Eq. 10 uniquely defines the exponent-three scalar `ω` factor for `tilde V_i`. Eq. 11 preserves a different glyph: its middle multiplier is Latin `w(tilde V_i)`, while the same `bar_A(tilde V_i)` is overloaded on the left and low/middle right branches and prose says that `bar_A` symbol equals `R_i^-1`. The official author response says the Eq. 10 weighting function enters Eq. 11, closing that semantic relation without equating Greek `ω` and Latin `w`. It still introduces no distinct base and equivalent information symbols. Thus `bar_A_equivalent=w(tilde V_i) A_base`, `A_base=R_i^-1` is only a `PAPER_DERIVED` conventional split. The middle branch evaluates to zero at the inclusive `k1` boundary through the author-response-backed weighting-function relation, so the operational singular domain is `|tilde V_i|>=k1`; row deletion and a positive infinite-variance limit remain executable but unselected completions.

The rendered equations use two distinct Greek symbols. Eq. 10 uses `ω` (U+03C9 omega) for the per-component IGGIII weight. Eqs. 12–14 use `ϖ` (U+03D6 GREEK PI SYMBOL, varpi) for adaptive/robust branch fusion. Legacy OCR/prompt aliases `v` and `omega` may identify the fusion field at compatibility boundaries, but the canonical contract calls it varpi and never relabels it as Eq. 10 omega.

Three additional spellings are compatibility/errata aliases only and are not paper-direct symbols: `V_k` maps to canonical `bar_V_k` and explicitly not `V_hat_k`; `A_k` maps to `bar_A_k`; and `A(e_V_i)` maps to `bar_A(tilde_V_i)`. They preserve older shorthand without adding or closing any method semantic.

Eqs. 12–14 directly close the convex state formula, convex covariance formula, threshold `c=1`, and varpi values 0.85/0.15. Eq. 14 prints `|Delta_X_tilde_k|` in both conditions; its absolute-value glyph remains canonical even though the printed square-root statistic is nonnegative whenever defined. Both branch equations use identical `Xhat_(k/k-1)` and `P_(k/k-1)`, and the text says to calculate adaptive and robust filtering separately before fusion, so the same unmodified prior and separate branch results are closed. Runtime scheduling, fusion-before-feedback, single-reset versus branch-reset order, and reset-Jacobian placement remain unresolved. Cross-covariance is an interpretation limitation for executable Eq. 13, not an ambiguity in the printed formula.
