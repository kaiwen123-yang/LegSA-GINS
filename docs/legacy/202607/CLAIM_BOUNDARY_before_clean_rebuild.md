# Pre-CLEAN0 Claim Boundary Freeze

This file preserves the shape of the earlier claim boundary without importing old metrics or row-level conclusions. Every historical result is retired from active evidence by CLEAN0.

## Historical Boundaries That Remain Useful As Lessons

- Trace was evaluation-only and could not drive sign, offset, provider choice, tuning, or feedback.
- final_v23 was an external reference/baseline and never solver input.
- Go2 state, position, velocity, yaw, contact, and metadata were not truth; approved uses were weak-prior or diagnostic.
- Receiver IMU was not interchangeable with Go2 body IMU.
- Baseline heading was not body yaw; lateral geometry, antenna order, frame convention, and wrap-safe residuals had to be explicit.
- Provider presence did not prove source lineage, and status fallback did not prove a full backend.
- Old aggregates or reconstructed summaries were weaker than final-output row evidence.
- Source-aware weighting, multi-state QM, Raw Doppler, and Go2 effects could not be inflated into universal improvement claims.
- Design packages, provider counts, logger schemas, and blocked smoke gates did not prove complete active nine-factor FGO.
- No output substitution, output-only correction, per-case trace tuning, or metric-driven epoch deletion was allowed.

## Historical Claims Now Retired

All earlier numerical performance, degradation, generalization, ablation, comparison, and paper-readiness statements are `legacy_only`. They may be studied as history but cannot support the rebuilt paper. Fresh claims require clean raw hashes, provider lineage, independent paper-rebuild execution, final-output metric cross-checks, and reviewer approval.
