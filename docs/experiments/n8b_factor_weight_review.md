# N8B Factor Weight Review

N8B reviews residual scale and effective weight policy for:

- receiver position
- receiver velocity
- dual yaw
- raw Doppler velocity
- Go2 proprioceptive joint
- smoothness
- diagnostic candidate factors

The report records per-factor p50/p95/max residual proxies, effective weight-scale labels, over- or under-constrained suspects, stuck-at-zero or cap suspects, and diagnostic weight-policy recommendations.

This review does not tune weights against trace/final_v23 outputs and does not make performance claims.
