# N7C5 Factor Ranking

N7C5 ranks these candidate factors:

- Go2 horizontal velocity fixed_1p0
- Go2 roll/pitch strengthened candidate
- Go2 joint roll/pitch plus horizontal velocity
- foot kinematic velocity candidate
- contact probability weighting
- yaw-rate between-factor candidate
- relative odometry between-factor candidate

Scores combine data availability, frame clarity, physical plausibility,
cross-source consistency, EKF readiness, FGO readiness, risk level, and novelty.

The ranking selects candidates for N7C6/N8A review; it is not paper performance
evidence.
