# PAPER4A IEEE TIM Route

## Fit

IEEE Transactions on Instrumentation and Measurement is plausible if the paper emphasizes measurement quality management, source-role discipline, provider construction, reproducible evidence classification, and bounded diagnostics.

## Main Route

1. Position the contribution as dataset-role-aware GNSS/INS measurement management for legged robots.
2. Use BY2 120-case evidence and PAPER2A QA behavior as the main experimental backbone.
3. Use provider v4 and RTKLIB/RINEX reconstruction as measurement infrastructure evidence.
4. Use literature modules as appendix diagnostics, not as exact reproductions.
5. Put limitations directly in the main text instead of hiding blocked yaw/exact-reproduction gaps.

## Not Ready For

- A paper centered on body-yaw superiority.
- A paper claiming exact faithful reproduction of five external dual-antenna algorithms.
- A severe-GNSS high-precision robustness paper.
- A final_v23 superiority paper.

## Minimum Next Evidence Before Submission Draft Freeze

1. Human review of PAPER4A classifications.
2. Final figure selection from main-text candidates only.
3. A clean claim-boundary scan over the manuscript draft.
4. Decision on whether QA/PG remains design extension or moves to a later implemented branch.
